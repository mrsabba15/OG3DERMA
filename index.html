How to Use

1

Wet your face

Splash your face with normal-temperature water.

2

Take a small amount

A pea-sized amount of cleanser is enough for one use.

3

Massage gently

Work it into your skin in circular motions for 30–60 seconds.

4

Rinse thoroughly

Wash off completely with water until no residue remains.

5

Pat dry & moisturize

Follow up with your regular moisturizer to lock in hydration.

Use twice daily — Morning & Night☀ 🌙
